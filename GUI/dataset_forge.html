🧬 Dataset Forge

A character dataset from a few images (from 1): new outfits, places and framings that keep the face.

1Source and references

Click the images to use as reference (up to 4 go into the model; more only help the likeness check). Only people.

2Character sheet

Analyze writes the captions the references lack and describes the features that do not change (age, hair, eyes...). Uses the shared Qwen3-VL-8B captioner (~5 GB, downloaded the first time).

3Plan

Face likeness is not installed: the images are kept without checking the likeness. Enable it above the previews of the trainer (👤 Face likeness → Enable...).

One image per line: framing, outfit, place, light, angle and expression. Edit, add or delete lines freely; this text (with the trigger) is the caption of each image. Half indoors, half outdoors, mostly close-ups.

4Generate

FLUX.3 Klein (NF4). Fast: NoiseToNova's turbo LoRA (PDD), 4 or 8 steps (about 12 or 25 s per image), with higher likeness but a more made-up, contrasted look. Best: 12 to 50 steps with CFG 3.5 (16 steps about 85 s per image, 28 about 2.5 min), softer and more natural. The most natural images: clean anatomy, new poses and the framing of the plan; the face drifts a little more than with Fibo 2.0. Downloaded the first time (~12 GB) if the FLUX.3 Klein trainer has not done it yet. FLUX Kommunity License: non-commercial use; commercial use of the images only for Qualifying Users (see the license). The images under the minimum likeness go to _rejected.

Fibo 2.0 Dev (NF4), 30 steps with CFG 5 (about 5 min per image). The highest likeness in our tests and clean anatomy, with a slightly more retouched look. Downloaded the first time (~13 GB) if the Fibo 2.0 trainer has not done it yet. CC BY-NC 4.0: non-commercial use only. The images under the minimum likeness go to _rejected.

Qwen-Image 2.1 (NF4), fast and with high likeness, but bodies can be out of proportion and it tends to copy the pose of the reference. Best quality: 20 steps, cleaner anatomy and higher likeness (about 40-70 s per image). Fast: Viggle's turbo LoRA, 4 steps (about 20-30 s), more deformed bodies. Each try is checked with the face likeness. Downloaded the first time (~22 GB with the text encoder) if the Qwen-Image 2.1 trainer has not done it yet. Qwen Research License: non-commercial use only. The images under the minimum likeness go to _rejected.

FLUX.2 Klein 9B (NF4) with kalle07's turbo LoRA, 4 steps (about 20 s per image). Natural anatomy, but in our tests the face drifts more than with Qwen-Image 2.1 and it does not always follow the framing. Downloaded the first time (~9 GB) if the FLUX.2 Klein 9B trainer has not done it yet. FLUX Non-Commercial License. The images under the minimum likeness go to _rejected.

Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (NF4, Apache 2.0) with the Lightning LoRA, 4 steps per image; each try is checked with the face likeness. Downloaded the first time (~27 GB with the text encoder) if the Qwen-Image 2512 trainer has not done it yet. The images under the minimum likeness go to _rejected.
